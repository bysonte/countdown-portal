# Countdown Portal — Design System

## Product
Personal countdown for a friends trip to Punta Cana (13 Sep 2026). The photos are the room; the UI is a lantern over memory.

## Scene
Late night, looking at old trip photos together while waiting for the next Caribbean dawn.

## Color
- Background: `#06252f` lagoon night
- Text: `#f4fafb` foam
- Muted: `#c5dde3` mist
- Accent: `#f0c27a` warm sand
- Water: `#7ee7f0` lagoon (secondary)

## Type
- UI: Figtree
- Soul lines: Dancing Script
- Body 16px+, headings balanced, tabular nums on the clock

## Motion
- Focal: mosaic ken-burns + photo crossfade
- Supporting: one hero settle, gallery stagger cap, flip digits, lightbox spring
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Rules
- No emoji as structural icons
- Glass only as lantern over photos
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on body text
- Touch targets ≥ 44px
- Visible `:focus-visible`
